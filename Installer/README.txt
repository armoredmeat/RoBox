RoBox 0.9 (beta) - vocoder and talkbox effect
==============================================

Thanks for testing RoBox! It's a vocoder and talkbox in one plugin, with its own built-in
synth, so you don't need any routing: put it on a vocal track, pick a waveform and a key,
and you get a robot or talkbox voice. Made with electro, Miami bass and bass music in mind.

This is a test version. Please tell me what you think and anything that goes wrong.


WHAT GETS INSTALLED
-------------------
- RoBox VST3 plugin   -> C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\RoBox.vst3
- RoBox standalone app (optional) -> C:\Program Files\RoBox\RoBox.exe
  The app runs without a DAW: pick your mic and output under Options > Audio Settings.
  Use headphones, or you'll get feedback.

Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps.


"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"?
----------------------------
The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows may warn you.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if RoBox doesn't show up (it's an effect, by "RoBox Audio").
2. Put RoBox on a vocal track and play it back (or monitor your mic).
3. You'll hear a robot voice straight away. Try:
   - Vocoder / Talkbox at the top
   - the presets (arrows at the top right)
   - Key, Octave and Chord in the Carrier section
   - Mix at the bottom right, to blend in your dry voice
4. Point at any control: the bar at the bottom of the window explains what it does.
   Hold Shift while turning a knob for fine control. Double-click a knob to reset it.

MIDI: switch on "MIDI" and send MIDI notes to the RoBox track, and the robot sings
the notes you play. In Ableton Live, make a MIDI track and set its "MIDI To" to the
vocal track, then choose RoBox in the box below.

Your own presets are saved in Documents\RoBox\Presets (easy to share).
